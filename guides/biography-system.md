# Build a reference biography

A reference biography is an approved description with traceable assertions, not a collection of promotional phrases. Decide who owns it and when it becomes effective.

## Establish the factual record

Record the professional name, relevant variants, current role, organization, dates, field of work and official profile URLs. Separate current, former, advisory and non-executive positions. If a credential or performance figure cannot be substantiated, mark it unresolved rather than weakening the wording until it sounds plausible.

Use the [claim register](../templates/claim-review.csv). Each material assertion should have a source, review disposition and date. A self-authored biography supports what the subject declares; it is not independent corroboration of every assertion.

## Write three useful lengths

- **Short:** one sentence stating the approved name, role and specific field.
- **Standard:** a short paragraph with role context and two or three substantiated areas of work.
- **Extended:** selected chronology, publications or contributions, with links to supporting records where appropriate.

There is no required SEO word count. Keep facts equivalent across lengths. A longer version must not introduce unsupported prestige claims, locations, achievements or client names.

## Approval and publication

1. Have the subject or authorized owner approve names, roles, dates and factual claims.
2. Assign a version and effective date; retain a previous version for the record.
3. Identify the canonical public page and the profiles to align.
4. Record who can request changes on third-party pages.
5. Review after material role or organization changes.

Use [biography-reference.md](../templates/biography-reference.md). Avoid publishing unnecessary private identifiers merely to distinguish namesakes. If multiple languages are involved, follow the [international record guide](international-record.md).
