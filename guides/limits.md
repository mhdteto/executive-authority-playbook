# Boundaries

The playbook organizes identity and source work. It does not certify truth, provide legal advice or guarantee removal of content, indexing, rankings, citations, recommendations or commercial outcomes.

A reference biography is approved input; it can still contain errors. Supporting source labels require human review. Several pages repeating one announcement do not become independent corroboration merely because their URLs differ.

AI responses vary by model, interface, retrieval, prompt, language, location and date. Changes in sampled answers do not by themselves prove that an intervention caused improvement.

All scenarios are fictional. They contain no real client results, verified benchmark, claimed market leadership or proprietary engagement record. Public and private evidence should be separated according to actual permissions and sensitivity.

A real engagement can require legal, privacy, employment or other specialist advice. Keep the status of each claim and correction explicit, including unknowns and declined requests.
