# Maintain an international executive record

A consistent international record does not require identical text in every language. It requires factual equivalence, approved terminology and clear source relationships.

## Identity and language

Keep a canonical professional name and explicitly approved variants, including transliterations where needed. Preserve meaningful diacritics. Do not assume a shortened name, translated title or altered organization suffix is interchangeable in every market.

Record the language, script, market, intended audience, approved role wording, reviewer and effective date in the [language review register](../templates/language-review.csv). If acceptable aliases differ by market, use separate configurations when running the Entity Consistency Auditor; its aliases apply to all observations within one run.

## Source context

Select sources according to the assertion: official organization records for appointments, original publications for authorship, appropriate registers for relevant credentials, and independent reporting for external descriptions. Registry availability and legal meaning vary; this playbook does not certify local compliance or credentials.

## Dates and locations

Use unambiguous dates (YYYY-MM-DD where known). Distinguish residence, office, employer location, audience market and remote service coverage. A market page must not imply a physical office, team or client that has not been established.

## Publication review

Check that all versions agree on current versus historical roles, dates and supported claims. Link official profiles carefully; an official URL is a reference, not proof that every external platform accepts the declared relationship.
