# Manage a role transition

An executive can hold several legitimate roles at once. A role transition should preserve chronology, not erase history to make every page look identical.

## Build the timeline

Record the role, organization, relationship type, start/end dates, official announcement and approved wording. Use exact dates only when supported. Unknown dates stay unknown. Do not infer employment from a partnership, appearance, investment or advisory mention.

## Separate records to update

- **Current owned biography:** reflect the effective role and indicate former roles where relevant.
- **Professional profile:** update the correct position and preserve the previous position's end date.
- **Organization page:** ask the responsible owner to confirm the relationship.
- **Historic article or conference page:** assess whether it is a historical record rather than an error.
- **AI answer:** capture its wording, date, interface and cited sources; first investigate the underlying record it may be using.

A conference page describing the person correctly at its publication date may not require rewriting. A present-tense directory entry carrying an old role may require correction. Explain that distinction in the review queue.

## Completion criteria

Approved owned pages reflect the new record; correction requests are tracked; unresolved third-party pages remain listed. Do not report that the internet or all AI systems have updated merely because the main biography changed.
