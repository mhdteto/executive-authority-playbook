# Request a factual source correction

Use this process for a concrete factual error. Disagreement with criticism, an unfavorable assessment or lawful historical reporting is a different matter and may require specialist advice.

## Prepare the record

For each disputed statement, preserve the source URL, access date, quoted location, current wording, proposed factual correction and supporting evidence. Explain why the evidence concerns the same entity and time period. Include contrary evidence rather than selecting only favorable material.

## Send a narrow request

Address the appropriate publisher or profile owner through its published process. State the precise correction, provide a minimal evidence trail and identify the authorized requester. Keep sensitive records out of a public issue or repository; use an appropriate private channel where necessary.

The [correction request template](../templates/correction-request.md) is a draft for human review. This playbook sends no messages, files no complaints and makes no legal demand.

## Track outcomes honestly

Statuses can include prepared, submitted, acknowledged, accepted, partly accepted, declined and unresolved. Record dates and actual outcomes. A submitted request is not a completed correction; a corrected page is not proof that caches, snippets or AI answers have changed.

If a publisher declines, record the reason and decide whether additional evidence or an appropriate specialist is needed. Do not create coordinated reports, artificial endorsements or fabricated counter-sources.
