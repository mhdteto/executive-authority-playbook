# Review a possible namesake collision

An answer about the wrong person is an identity problem; a different spelling alone does not prove a collision.

## Preserve the observation

Record the exact query or prompt, language, market, engine/interface, date and full answer or search observation. Keep the source URLs and the passages where identities seem mixed. A search snippet and the underlying page may differ; record which one was inspected.

## Compare discriminating facts

Use public professional attributes relevant to the question: organization, role period, field, publications and official profile relationships. Distinguish evidence of a match from a shared name. Avoid adding sensitive personal identifiers to the public record just to resolve ambiguity.

Classify each source as intended entity, different entity or unresolved, with the review rationale. A partial overlap or missing information remains unresolved. The [namesake log](../templates/namesake-review.csv) records this without merging people automatically.

## Correct at the appropriate source

If an owned biography is ambiguous, clarify its supported context and official links. If a third-party page mixes records, make a specific evidence-backed request. If only an AI answer mixes the records, save the observation and assess the cited sources before attributing the error to one page.

Repeated prompts can produce different answers. A single successful follow-up does not establish that a collision is permanently resolved. Keep the measured conditions and any unresolved examples.
